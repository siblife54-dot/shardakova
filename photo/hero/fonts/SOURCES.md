# Quote typefaces

Self-hosted WOFF2 subsets from the official Google Fonts repository,
licensed under the SIL Open Font License. License texts are included here.
The page does not contact a font service.

- `story-roman.woff2` and `story-italic.woff2`: Cormorant Garamond Regular and
  Italic, static weight 500, Latin/Cyrillic and punctuation.
  Source: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/cormorantgaramond
- `campaign-sans.woff2`: Manrope variable, Latin/Cyrillic and punctuation.
  The quote uses weights 400–600.
  Source: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/manrope

Subsets were exported with fontTools. Complete license texts are retained
in `cormorant-OFL.txt` and `manrope-OFL.txt`.
